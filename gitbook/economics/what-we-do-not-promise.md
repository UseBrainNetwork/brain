# What we do not promise

A short page, on purpose.

**No returns.** Running a node pays a share of an hourly pool that the operator sets and funds. The pool can be any number including zero. The fee flow that funds it depends on trading volume nobody controls. Rewards are not guaranteed, not projected, and not an investment.

**No token price.** Nothing on the site or in this book states, implies or models a price for the BRAIN token. The economics pages show SOL that moved and SOL that is owed.

**No emissions.** No tokens are created to pay anyone. The pool is SOL from fees already earned.

**No staking.** Holding tokens earns nothing by itself. The holding multiplier is applied to verified compute, and multiplies zero into zero.

**No passive income.** Uptime without verified work earns nothing. A tab that is open but never verifies a unit earns nothing.

**No points, quests, levels, licences, NFTs or governance.** They have been suggested, with real enthusiasm, and the answer is no. Each would either pay for something that is not compute or create a second thing to speculate on.

**No savings claims.** BRAIN has not measured its cost against anyone else's and does not say it is cheaper.

**No "verified" where it is not.** Node inference receipts say `node-reported`. Upstream receipts say `unverified-provider-response`. Only browser work the server checked bit-exactly, and inference stages two tabs agreed on, say verified.

**No uptime guarantee.** BRAIN has had two multi-hour outages in its first three days. The [incident log](../trust/incident-log.md) is public.

What BRAIN does promise is narrower and checkable: the rules on these pages are the rules in the code, the figures on the site come from records you can read, and when something is not real yet, it is labelled.
